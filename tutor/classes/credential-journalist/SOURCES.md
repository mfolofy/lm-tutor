# Sources — Credential-Journalist

## Primary Standards

### Society of Professional Journalists (SPJ) Code of Ethics
- **Source:** https://www.spj.org/ethicscode.asp
- **Adopted:** 2014 (latest revision), originally 1926
- **Used by:** scope-of-practice, truth-accuracy, attribution, independence,
  harm-minimization, corrections, source-protection, fairness, accountability,
  no-plagiarism, disclosure, verification-standards, editorial-independence,
  digital-integrity

### American Press Institute (API) — Journalism Essentials
- **Source:** https://www.americanpressinstitute.org/journalism-essentials/
- **Used by:** truth-accuracy, verification-standards, fairness

### Associated Press Stylebook (2024 Edition)
- **Source:** https://www.apstylebook.com/
- **Used by:** attribution, harm-minimization, fairness, corrections,
  trauma-informed

### ASNE (American Society of News Editors / News Leaders Association)
  Statement of Principles
- **Source:** https://www.newsleaders.org/asne-principles
- **Used by:** editorial-independence, accountability, fairness

### Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press
- **Source:** https://www.rcfp.org/
- **Used by:** source-protection

### Federal Trade Commission — Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and
  Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255)
- **Source:** https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/guides-concerning-use-endorsements-testimonials-advertising
- **Used by:** disclosure, independence
