# SOURCES — credential-dentist (Dental Professional Standards)

All rules in `class.yaml` derive from the ADA Code of Ethics, CDC/OSHA dental
guidelines, HIPAA, DEA regulations, ALARA principles, and established
dental-legal doctrines. Every rule cites its specific source. No rule exists
without a source.

## Primary sources

### ADA Code of Ethics (2025)
- **American Dental Association Principles of Ethics and Code of Professional
  Conduct** — Current edition.
- <https://www.ada.org/about/principles/code-of-ethics>
- **Key principles cited:**
  - Section 1 — Patient Autonomy (informed consent, treatment refusal)
  - Section 2 — Nonmaleficence (do no harm, standard of care)
  - Section 3 — Beneficence (duty to refer, emergency care)
  - Section 4 — Justice (fair treatment, non-discrimination)
  - Section 5 — Veracity (truthful communication, records accuracy)
  - Section 5.B — Representation of Care (accurate treatment documentation)
  - Section 5.E — Second Opinions and Consultations
  - Section 5.F — Mandatory Reporting
  - Section 5.G — Patient Records
  - Section 4.D — Professional Relations (boundaries, referrals)

### CDC Guidelines for Infection Control in Dental Health-Care Settings
- **Centers for Disease Control and Prevention** — Summary of Infection
  Prevention Practices in Dental Settings: Basic Expectations for Safe Care
  (current edition).
- <https://www.cdc.gov/oralhealth/infectioncontrol/guidelines/>
- **Key provisions:** Standard precautions, hand hygiene, PPE, sterilization
  and disinfection of patient-care items, environmental infection control,
  dental unit water quality, program evaluation.
- **MMWR Recommendations and Reports** — "Guidelines for Infection Control in
  Dental Health-Care Settings — 2003" and subsequent updates.
- **Key sections:** 5 (Sterilization and Disinfection of Patient-Care Items),
  6 (Environmental Infection Control), 7 (Dental Unit Waterlines), 8 (Program
  Evaluation).

### OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens Standard
- **Occupational Safety and Health Administration**, 29 CFR § 1910.1030 —
  Bloodborne Pathogens.
- <https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1030>
- **Key provisions:** Exposure control plan, engineering and work practice
  controls, PPE, hepatitis B vaccination, post-exposure evaluation and
  follow-up, communication of hazards, recordkeeping.
- **Enforcement:** OSHA regional offices. Civil penalties for violations.

### HIPAA Privacy Rule
- **Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA)** —
  Privacy Rule, 45 CFR § 164.500–534.
- <https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/index.html>
- **Key provisions:** Definition of PHI (§ 164.501), minimum necessary standard
  (§ 164.502(b)), permitted uses and disclosures (§ 164.506–508), authorization
  requirements (§ 164.508), de-identification standard (§ 164.514).
- **Enforcement:** HHS Office for Civil Rights (OCR). Civil penalties up to
  $50,000 per violation, tiered by culpability (42 U.S.C. § 1320d-5).

### DEA Controlled Substances Act
- **Controlled Substances Act**, 21 U.S.C. § 801–971.
- **DEA Regulations**, 21 CFR Part 1300–1321.
- <https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/>
- **Key provisions:** Schedules II-V controlled substances, prescription
  requirements, registration, recordkeeping, security requirements.
- **Common dental controlled substances:** Schedule III (acetaminophen with
  codeine, ketorolac), Schedule IV (benzodiazepines for anxiety, tramadol),
  Schedule V (codeine-containing cough syrups).
- **Ryan Haight Act** (21 U.S.C. § 802) — In-person evaluation required for
  controlled substance prescriptions via telemedicine.

### ALARA Principles
- **ADA/FDA Selection Criteria for Dental Radiography** — Joint publication,
  current edition.
- <https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/medical-x-ray-imaging/dental-radiographic-examinations>
- <https://www.ada.org/resources/ada-library/oral-health-topics/x-rays>
- **Key principles:** As Low As Reasonably Achievable, clinical justification
  for every radiograph, use of fastest image receptors, appropriate collimation,
  lead aprons and thyroid collars, patient-specific selection criteria.

### State Dental Practice Acts
- **Federation of State Medical Boards** — State-specific regulations vary.
  Key areas generally regulated: scope of practice, supervision requirements
  for dental hygienists and assistants, anesthesia permits, delegation rules,
  record retention, mandatory continuing education.
- **ADEA Compendium** — State regulatory requirements for dental professionals.

## Coverage honesty

**Checkable rules (6):** `scope-of-practice`, `HIPAA-confidentiality`,
`informed-consent`, `prescribing-boundaries`, `radiography`, `recordkeeping`.

These are codifiable as regex patterns that detect specific text-based
violations: AI posing as a dentist, exposure of PHI identifiers, proceeding
without documented consent, specific medication dosages, ordering radiographs
without clinical justification, and retroactive record alteration.

They are high precision but narrow — they detect specific known-bad text
patterns but do not evaluate whether the AI's overall dental reasoning is sound.

**Teaching-only rules (9):** infection-control, patient-assessment, anesthesia,
mandatory-reporting, emergency-preparedness, professional-boundaries,
antibiotic-prophylaxis, treatment-planning, second-opinion-referral. These
require contextual or compositional judgment and are evaluated by the LLM
judge (Layer 2) or the Socratic fix step.

**What is NOT covered:** Full dental practice compliance requires state dental
board licensure, DEA registration, malpractice coverage, hospital privileges,
OSHA compliance programs, and hundreds of regulatory requirements. This class
teaches an LLM to recognize the most common dental professional standards
violations and to follow them at generation time. It does not replace a
compliance officer, risk manager, or licensed dentist.

## Verification

Each rule with `check_regex` is FAIL/PASS tested in
`tests/test_credential_dentist.py`. Teaching-only rules are tested for content
presence via syllabus loading. A rule cannot ship without its test pair.
