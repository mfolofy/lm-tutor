# SOURCES — credential-judge (Judicial Professional Standards)

All rules in `class.yaml` derive from the ABA Code of Judicial Conduct, the US
Constitution (5th, 6th, 14th Amendments), the Federal Rules of Evidence, the
Federal Rules of Civil and Criminal Procedure, the United States Sentencing
Guidelines, and related federal statutes. Every rule cites its specific source.
No rule exists without a source.

## Primary standards

### ABA Code of Judicial Conduct (2020)
- **American Bar Association, Model Code of Judicial Conduct (2020).**
  <https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_code_of_judicial_conduct/>
- Canons cited: 1 (Integrity and Independence), 2 (Avoiding Impropriety),
  3 (Impartial and Diligent Performance), 4 (Outside Activities).

### United States Constitution
- **Fifth Amendment** — Due Process Clause; no person shall be deprived of
  life, liberty, or property without due process of law; grand jury indictment;
  double jeopardy; self-incrimination.
- **Sixth Amendment** — Right to speedy and public trial, impartial jury,
  notice of charges, confrontation, compulsory process, assistance of counsel.
- **Fourteenth Amendment** — Due Process and Equal Protection Clauses
  applied to the states; incorporation of Bill of Rights against states.

### Federal Rules of Evidence (2024)
- **Federal Rules of Evidence.** <https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre>
- FRE 401-403 (Relevance); FRE 404 (Character Evidence);
  FRE 501 (Privileges); FRE 702-705 (Expert Testimony, *Daubert* standard);
  FRE 801-807 (Hearsay); FRE 901-902 (Authentication).

### Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (2024)
- **Federal Rules of Civil Procedure.** <https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcp>
- Rule 11 (Signing Pleadings), Rule 12 (Defenses and Objections),
  Rule 16 (Pretrial Conferences and Scheduling), Rule 37 (Failure to Make
  Disclosures/Sanctions), Rule 55 (Default), Rule 56 (Summary Judgment).

### Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure (2024)
- **Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure.** <https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcrmp>
- Rule 11 (Pleas), Rule 42 (Criminal Contempt).

### United States Sentencing Guidelines (USSG 2024)
- **United States Sentencing Commission, Guidelines Manual (2024).**
  <https://www.ussc.gov/guidelines>
- Chapter 1 (Authority), Chapter 2 (Offense Conduct), Chapter 4 (Criminal
  History), Chapter 5 (Determining the Sentence).
- 18 USC Sec. 3553(a) — Factors to be considered in imposing a sentence.
- 18 USC Sec. 3742 — Review of a sentence.

## Coverage honesty

**Checkable rules (6):** `scope-of-practice`, `judicial-impartiality`,
`ex-parte-communication`, `due-process`, `ethics`, `opinions`.

These are codifiable as regex patterns that detect specific text-based
violations: claiming judicial office, statements of bias or prejudgment,
inviting or conducting ex parte communications, denying procedural rights,
soliciting gifts or political endorsements, and issuing secret/unsigned
rulings without reasoning.

They are high precision but narrow — they detect specific known-bad text
patterns but do not evaluate whether the AI's overall judicial reasoning is
sound.

**Teaching-only rules (9):** Recusal, sentencing, evidence-rules, contempt,
jurisdiction, judicial-decorum, plea-agreements, administrative-authority,
and judicial-competence. These require contextual or compositional judgment
and are evaluated by the LLM judge (Layer 2) or the Socratic fix step.

**What is NOT covered:** Full judicial practice requires understanding of
per-circuit variations in local rules, substantive area knowledge (patent,
admiralty, bankruptcy), complex multi-district litigation management, and
the professional judgment required for nuanced ethical questions. This class
teaches an LLM to recognize the most common judicial professional standards
and to follow them at generation time. It does not certify the AI to serve
as a judge or replace a human judicial officer's professional judgment.

## Verification

Each rule with `check_regex` is FAIL/PASS tested in
`tests/test_credential_judge.py`. Teaching-only rules are tested for content
presence via syllabus loading. A rule cannot ship without its test pair.
